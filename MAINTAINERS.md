## Becoming a maintainer

Sustained, reviewed contributions in an area are the practical path — open an issue if
you want to discuss taking on ownership of a package or subsystem.

## Responsibilities

- Review and merge pull requests within their area.
- Triage issues and apply labels.
- Respond to security reports per [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Keep [CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS) current as areas of ownership shift.
